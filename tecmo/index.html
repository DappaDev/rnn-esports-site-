Touch & controllers

On phones and tablets the on-screen pad appears automatically; slide your thumb across the D-pad or between A and B. Turn it on or off with the ✚ button. Bluetooth or USB controllers work too: the first is Player 1, the second is Player 2.

Season progress saves automatically in this browser. Save/Load keeps one quick snapshot per device.
